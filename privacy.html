Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Oficina Clipping is used only by its owner with his own TikTok accounts.

Data we access

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic).
  • Account stats: followers, following, likes and video count (user.info.stats).
  • Public videos of the connected account and their views, likes, comments and shares (video.list).
  • Permission to upload and post videos that the owner chooses (video.upload, video.publish).

How we use it

Only to post the owner's clips when he asks and to show him how his videos perform. Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only in the macOS Keychain of the owner's computer. Video stats are kept in the owner's private control panel.

Deletion

Revoking access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) stops all access. The owner can delete the stored tokens and stats at any time.
