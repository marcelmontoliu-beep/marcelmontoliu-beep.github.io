Oficina Clipping

Oficina Clipping is a personal desktop tool for a gaming content creator. It edits vertical clips on the creator's own Mac and shows them in a private control panel, where the creator reviews each clip and decides whether to post it to his own TikTok account.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit: the creator connects his own TikTok accounts with TikTok login.
  • Content Posting API: for each clip, the creator edits the caption, chooses who can see the video, sets comments, duets and stitches, can disclose commercial content, and presses "Publicar en TikTok". Nothing is posted without that action.
  • Stats: the creator sees follower counts and the views, likes, comments and shares of his own videos.
